Where this data comes from

Scraped once from two official PolyU websites.

  • Campus Map — 561 POIs with waypoints and trilingual names
    polyu.edu.hk/campus-map
  • GreenMap@PolyU — 315 recycling, water and green facilities
    polyu.edu.hk/greenmap

Walkway links are hand-authored on top of published coordinates and may contain errors. Stairs and lifsts are not modelled. Opening hours are quoted only where PolyU publishes them.
